📄 Papers

Your Transformer Can Hold Two Thoughts at Once (Linear Superposition Hypothesis)
The paper shows that when inputs from distinct text streams are linearly combined, model outputs are a superposition of individual next-token distributions, and that superposition is intrinsic to the Transformer architecture rather than an emergent training effect.
Rufus-Air: An Open LLM Post-Training Recipe
An open, reproducible post-training recipe on GLM-4.5-Air-Base (106B-A12B) with eight serial stages (SFT → Reasoning RL → Coding RL → Instruction-Following RL → General/Coding/Search Agent → RLHF), fully documenting data, reward design, and infrastructure.
AgentKernel: The Trust-Native Agentic Operating System
Argues current governance stacks are application-level middleware sharing a process trust boundary with the agents they monitor, and that the trust boundary must move down into the OS to isolate untrusted inputs, long-term memory, and privileged tool calls.
Learning to Discover Interesting Mathematics
Defines a theorem's "intrinsic interestingness" as the ratio between proof length and statement length, to filter genuinely valuable results out of LLM-generated math at scale.
Capable yet Parsimonious: Extracting Hidden Chain-of-Thought in Frontier Models
By registering a simple custom tool through a standard API feature, the authors induce closed frontier models to externalize intermediate reasoning, validating against native CoT on open-source models.
