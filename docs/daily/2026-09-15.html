Pelican-Sim 1.0: a general world model simulator for embodied intelligence

ReactHuman: a physics-grounded benchmark for human-like reactive decision-making
Targets sudden physical hazards like catching a slipping plate or dodging a falling knife, testing whether multimodal LLMs can turn physical understanding into immediate reaction.
SAS: simple attention sparsification via end-to-end optimization of context ranking
Argues existing trainable sparsification methods can only distill layer-wise dense attention because hard Top-K blocks gradients, and instead optimizes context ranking end to end.
Breaking the Vision-Action Shortcut: latent interface training for generalizable robot foundation models
Shows models exploit task-irrelevant visual cues correlated with demonstrated actions in the training distribution, and constrains how visual information enters action generation to mitigate degradation under distribution shift.
StepAudio 3 Gen technical report: a unified audio generation framework
A discrete autoregressive generator over RVQ tokens that supports zero-shot TTS, voice design, vocal generation, sound effects, music and mixtures within one framework, departing from the dominant diffusion Transformer route.
Benchmark Radar: a living database and search engine for AI benchmarks
Daily discovery of benchmark papers, with datasets and code located and the settings behind reported scores explained, covering LLMs, agents, coding, reasoning and safety.
COBRA-Skills: contextual bandit-guided evolution for agent skill optimization
Formulates skill optimization as budgeted sequential optimization over a dynamically evolving candidate space, using contextual bandits for prioritization to reduce reliance on costly execution-based evaluation and large task datasets.
DataFlex-RL: an evaluation platform for RLVR data policies
