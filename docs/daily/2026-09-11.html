💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that agent infrastructure is being absorbed by platforms in one sweep—OpenAI's Agents API, GPT-Live-1, Meta's Muse payment loop, and Slack's generative UI are all draining the value of building your own orchestration layer. Meanwhile, DeepSeek V4.1-Flash cuts long-context cost to a quarter with FP4 KV Cache, and Ramp data showing per-employee AI spend down 10% and token prices down 41% confirms inference economics are deflating fast, forcing model vendors to make up in volume what they lose in price. When Terence Tao starts worrying that AI will exhaust open problems non-renewably, it may be time to reconsider: the bottleneck on capability growth may not be compute, but trust and oversight.
