📄 Papers

Negative Self-Distillation: Learning to Reason by Avoiding Flaws
The study finds On-Policy Self-Distillation can severely degrade complex reasoning by forcing the student to imitate artificially confident traces conditioned on privileged information; the paper proposes a negative self-distillation paradigm to correct it.
An Open Recipe for IMO Gold: Training Nemotron for Olympiad Mathematics
Starting from Nemotron 3 Ultra, two specialist checkpoints are trained with SFT and RL, plus a fully natural-language test-time-compute pipeline with no formal prover, external tools, or internet.
NCP-ArchPreview: Moving towards Latent Space Language Models through Next Concept Prediction
Beyond standard next-token prediction, the model learns to predict discrete concepts spanning multiple tokens, building a product-quantized concept vocabulary while preserving token-level autoregressive generation.
T1: Terminal Agent Reinforcement Learning for Long-Horizon Tasks
A 122B MoE model operating a real shell in a cloud sandbox for up to 300+ tool-call turns per task, rewarded by each task's own verifier, with a full recipe for stabilizing actor-critic training.
PARSER: Read in Parallel, Reason in Depth for Long-Context LLM Agents
Decouples reading from reasoning — a bank of lightweight subagents each bound to a single chunk reads the whole document in parallel while a lead agent reasons in depth, breaking the linear tie between document length and inference latency.
IdeaAMBIG: Benchmarking Implementation-Critical Gaps in Research-Idea Specifications
Studies whether a novel, coherent research idea is specified well enough for a competent implementer or coding agent to build the intended method without unsupported assumptions, constructing evidence-grounded specifications and their supported rewrites.
MaP-WAM: World-Action Modeling with Memory-Grounded Planning
For inherently non-Markovian long-horizon manipulation, proposes a Memory-as-Plans framework that avoids losing fine-grained visual evidence or trading off history coverage against execution efficiency, unlike language summaries or growing visual windows.
SenseNova-U1.5: Towards Native Unified Visual Intelligence
An 8B-MoT native unified multimodal model that understands, reasons about, and generates visual content in an encoder-free, VAE-free architecture, supporting native resolutions up to 4K and strengthened via spatially coherent patch reconstruction.
HyQuant: Hybrid-Precision Quantization for LLM Attention
Unlike mainstream smoothing techniques for handling outliers, HyQuant proposes a hybrid quantization design that better balances accuracy and efficiency in the attention module at very low bit-widths.
Google DeepMind AlphaGenome Atlas: A predictive map of every possible DNA letter change in the human genome
Maps the molecular effects of 9 billion single-letter DNA variants across the human genome.
