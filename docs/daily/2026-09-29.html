📄 Papers

D-JEPA: A Decision-Aligned Latent World Model
Identifies a "decision-local prediction gap" — a candidate future predicted closer to the goal can produce a worse realized outcome than an available alternative; D-JEPA learns decision-relevant relations among candidate futures to align the latent space.
Disaggregated Quantization: Specializing LLM Prefill and Decode
Proposes DQ, specializing computation formats, weights, and storage placement for prefill (low-precision arithmetic for speed) and decode (compact weights to reduce memory traffic), validated on Qwen 3 and Gemma 3.
Block Sparse Attention with Log-Linear Complexity (PISA)
Proposes a pyramid Top-K selection strategy for block-sparse attention, solving the quadratic complexity of conventional block selection that requires scoring all query-block pairs.
InternW0-Δ: A World Action Model with 20K+ Hours of Open Data
A unified WAM jointly modeling visual dynamics and action generation, integrating visual dynamics, scene semantics, geometry, and motion priors from large-scale pretrained models.
AgentWorld: Benchmarking Long-Horizon Collaboration of Multi-agent LLMs
Includes 100 human-annotated tasks (+100 augmented variants) spanning 50+ interaction rounds in an MMORPG sandbox, requiring 3-20 agents with asymmetric collaboration, filling a gap in evaluating genuine collaboration capabilities.
Softmax Reparameterization for Output-Head Quantization
Proposes a post-training method that selects a functionally equivalent output head before quantization by subtracting a scalar multiple of the vocabulary-row mean, significantly reducing inference cost from large-vocabulary output heads.
SAGE: Mitigating Long-Horizon Reasoning Biases via Topological Guidance
Introduces Symbolic Closure Analysis (SCA) as a theoretical lens, identifying exploration bias and compounding bias as two failure modes, and improving long-horizon reasoning under sparse rewards via topological guidance.
