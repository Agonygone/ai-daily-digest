💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is the collective maturation of agent infrastructure: OpenAI shipped the Agents API, GPT-Live-1 voice API, and a Data Agent in one day, Meta put Muse inside WhatsApp, and Slack lets you generate apps right in a chat window—agents are no longer demos but product skeletons. Meanwhile DeepSeek V4.1-Flash cuts KV cache to a quarter under an MIT license, and Ramp data showing per-employee AI spend down nearly 10% with token prices down 41% since March confirms the capability-up, price-down double punch is real. A darker thread runs alongside: Anthropic's distillation allegations, OpenAI's math data dispute, and a departing researcher's extinction warning all hit headlines the same day, repricing the relationship between frontier labs, academia, and regulators. For builders, the highest-leverage work right now isn't training another model—it's nailing the harness, routing, and evaluation layers, and today's OmniRoute, superpowers, and SWE-Bench Pro Verified each claim one of those corners.
